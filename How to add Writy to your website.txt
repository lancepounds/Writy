HOW TO ADD THE INSTALLABLE WRITY TO YOUR WEBSITE

There are 8 files in this folder. All 8 go in the same folder on your website
as your current writy.html. The new writy.html replaces the old one.

  writy.html                 Writy itself (new version)
  writy.webmanifest          Tells devices Writy's name and icon, so it can be installed
  writy-sw.js                The offline helper: keeps a copy of Writy on the device
  writy-32.png, writy-192.png, writy-512.png,
  writy-maskable-512.png, writy-apple-180.png    The app icons

PUT THEM ON GITHUB
  1. Go to github.com and open the repository for your website.
  2. Open the folder that has writy.html in it.
  3. Click "Add file", then "Upload files".
  4. Drag all 8 files from this folder into the page.
  5. Click "Commit changes".
  6. Wait a minute or two while GitHub updates your website.

INSTALL WRITY
  First open writy.html on your website once while you are online.
  - Computer (Chrome or Edge): open the Start menu in Writy and choose
    "Install Writy (works offline)", or click the install button at the
    right end of the address bar.
  - iPad or iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen",
    then "Add".
  - Mac (Safari): choose File, then "Add to Dock", then "Add".
  - Android (Chrome): open the browser menu and choose "Install app".

After that, Writy opens from its own icon and works without internet.

YOUR DOCUMENTS IN THE INSTALLED WRITY
  - Installed from Chrome or Edge (on a computer or Android): the same
    documents as on the website.
  - Installed on an iPad, iPhone, or a Mac with Safari: Apple keeps installed
    web apps apart from Safari, so the installed Writy starts fresh, with just
    the example document. To bring yours along: in Safari, open Backup and
    choose "Copy backup text". Then open the installed Writy, open Backup,
    paste it in the box and choose "Restore from pasted text".

LATER UPDATES
  Just upload the new writy.html the same way. Writy picks it up the next time
  it opens while you are online. The other 7 files only need uploading once.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - The offline helper only looks after Writy's own files. The rest of your
    website is not affected.
  - Email, online voices (like the Google voices in Chrome) and webcam eye
    tracking still need internet. Everything else works offline.
